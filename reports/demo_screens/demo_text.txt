APR 2015 | Worse loans begin. | From 2015Q2, borrowers with the same score default more: at least 1.2× the 2012–13 month-12 rate. Their month-12 outcomes are 18 months away, so nothing can show it yet.
2015–2018 | The stability index stays flat. | PSI compares who applies, not how they repay. For the champion it never went above 0.057 in any quarter to 2018, far under its 0.10 alarm line.
JUN 2017 | The first realistic alarm. | The as-of model, built only on data known at the end of 2014, first crosses 1.10 here: 26 months after the first worse loans, with 687,800 loans booked meanwhile. The reference model, trained on outcomes a lender could not yet have seen, would have rung in Dec 2016.

MODEL RISK CASE STUDY · LENDINGCLUB · 2.26 MILLION LOANS
The alarm that never rang.

A credit model built on 2012–13 loans kept ranking borrowers well and passed every stability check through 2018. Underneath, defaults ran far above its expectations. A lender relying on it would have found out about two years after the first worse loans went out.
